Maui

Maui is the deuteragonist of Disney's Moana franchise. He is a boisterous demigod of South Pacific legend who predominately serves as a guardian of mankind. Along with his immortality and superhuman strength, Maui is equipped with a giant fish hook which grants him magical abilities, such as shapeshifting. Tales surrounding Maui's exploits and accomplishments have been embedded into mythologies across Polynesia, and enshrined as animated tattoos tapestried across the demigod's skin.

Te Fiti

Te Fiti is a major character in Disney's 2016 animated feature film Moana. She is a goddess with the power to create life—an ability that she used to create the islands of Polynesia. After her heart had been stolen by Maui, she became Te Kā, a demon of earth and fire and the main antagonist of the film. With Te Kā in her place, the islands that Te Fiti created were slowly consumed by a life-killing darkness that could only be vanquished by returning Te Fiti's heart to its rightful place.

Heihei

Heihei (sometimes spelled Hei Hei) is a major character in the Moana franchise. He is a rooster from Motunui, known for being stupid and clueless. Heihei’s idiocy is a common cause for trouble throughout Moana’s adventures, but in a roundabout way, can also guide her down the right path.

Pua

Pua is a supporting character in the Moana franchise. He is a kunekune pig and Moana's childhood best friend. Despite his innocence and lack of courage, Pua is a loyal companion who steadfastly accompanies Moana on her adventures across the seas.

Blobfish (Moana)

The Blobfish is a minor character that appears in Disney's 2024 animated feature film Moana 2. It is a peculiar monster that resides with the Kakamora and is the source of the neurotoxin for their blowdarts.

Chief Tui

Chief Tui is a supporting character in the Moana franchise. He is the village leader of Motunui, where he resides alongside his wife, Sina, and daughters, Moana and Simea. Descended from a long line of chieftans, Tui takes great pride in his homeland and aspires to someday have Moana follow in his footsteps.

Eight-Eyed Bats

The Eight-Eyed Bats are characters that are featured in the 2016 Disney animated feature film Moana.

Gramma Tala

Gramma Tala is a major character in the Moana franchise. She was a wise, but eccentric storyteller of Motunui, where she was ridiculed as the "village crazy lady" for her beliefs. Tala dedicated the final years of her life to mentoring her eldest granddaughter, Moana, in the cultural traditions of their people. Upon Tala’s sudden passing, she was reincarnated as a manta ray and continued to guide Moana throughout her adventures.

Kakamora

The Kakamora are major characters of Disney's Moana franchise. Described as “murdering little pirates” by Maui, the Kakamora are a tribe of coconuts who sail the seas on fleets of flotsam and jetsam.

Kele

Kele is a supporting character in Disney's 2024 animated feature film Moana 2. He is a cantankerous farmer from Motunui who becomes a reluctant member of Moana's wayfinding crew to cultivate and maintain their food supply.

Kotu

Kotu is a supporting character in Disney's 2024 animated feature film Moana 2. He is a Kakamora warrior and assassin who forms an alliance with Moana to break the curse surrounding the island of Motufetu.

Loto

Loto (short for Lototasi) is a supporting character in Disney's 2024 animated feature film Moana 2. She is a 16-year-old inventor and engineer from Motunui who joins Moana's wayfinding crew to design and maintains the canoe.

Matai Vasa

Matai Vasa is a minor character in Disney's Moana franchise. He is the ancestral leader of Moana's tribe who held the title of “Tautai” before her, having led their tribe to Motunui.

Matangi

Matangi is a supporting character in Disney's 2024 animated feature film Moana 2. She is a crafty and mysterious demigoddess who serves as an enforcer for Nalo, the storm god.

Mini Maui

Mini Maui is a supporting character in the Moana franchise. He is a sentient tattoo belonging to the demigod Maui.

Moni

Moni is a supporting character in Disney's 2024 animated feature film Moana 2. He is a 19-year old storyteller from Motunui with a passion for its history and myths, particularly those involving the demigod Maui. Moni becomes a member of Moana's wayfinding crew to document their adventures.

Motunui Villagers

The Motunui Villagers are background characters from Disney's 2016 animated feature film Moana and its 2024 sequel. They are a group of villagers who live in the isle of Motunui.

Nalo

Nalo is the main antagonist in Disney's 2024 animated feature film Moana 2. He is an envious storm god who has cursed and guards the mysterious island of Motufetu for centuries with a menacing storm barrier of clouds, lightning, and tornadoes. Nalo is also described as a substantially more fearsome threat than what Moana has faced before.[1] It is said that Nalo gains strength and power by severing the connection of the people of the ocean.

Ocean

The ocean is a supporting character in the Moana franchise. A magical entity, the ocean has a high level of anthropomorphism that allows it to interact with the world around it. When the islands of Polynesia fall victim to a devastating curse, the ocean partners up with a young islander named Moana to end the plight. Over the course of the journey, the ocean becomes one of Moana's most trusted and significant companions.

Simea

Simea is a supporting character in Disney's 2024 animated feature film Moana 2. She is a 3-year-old girl who is the youngest daughter of Chief Tui and Sina and the younger sister of Moana.

Sina

Sina is a character in the Moana franchise. She is the wife of Chief Tui and the mother of Moana and Simea.

Tamatoa

Tamatoa is the secondary antagonist of Disney's 2016 animated feature film Moana. He is a villainous giant crab who covets all things shiny and valuable. In an effort to exude beauty and superiority, Tamatoa displays his collected treasures atop his golden shell.
